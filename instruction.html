What is the Simon Game?

The Simon Game is a classic memory game where players must remember and repeat a growing sequence of colors (and sounds). With every correct round, the sequence becomes longer and more challenging.

Objective

The goal of the game is to correctly repeat the sequence shown by the game. The longer you survive, the higher your score.

How to Play

  • Press the Start button (or any key, depending on the version).
  • The game will flash one color to begin the sequence.
  • Click or tap the same color shown by the game.
  • If correct, the game adds one more color to the sequence.
  • Repeat the entire sequence in the correct order.
  • The game continues until you make a mistake.

Game Rules

  • You must follow the exact order of colors.
  • One wrong click ends the game.
  • The sequence increases by one color each round.

Tips to Improve Your Score

  • Focus carefully on the pattern before clicking.
  • Try to memorize the sequence in chunks.
  • Stay calm—rushing often causes mistakes.

End of the Game

When you press a wrong color, the game ends and your final score is displayed. You can restart the game and try to beat your previous score.
